# TECH_SALES_ANALYTICS
Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard — Project Description

Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard is an interactive data analysis dashboard designed to provide a comprehensive overview of mobile sales performance. The dashboard brings together key sales metrics, customer ratings, payment methods, mobile models, daily/monthly trends, and geographical distribution in a single interactive view.

 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The top section provides four important KPIs:

Total Sales – 68.97M: Shows the overall sales generated.
Total Transactions – 324: Represents the total number of transactions.
Total Units Sold – 2K: Displays the total quantity of mobile units sold.
Average – 39.87K: Shows the average sales value.

These KPIs give a quick snapshot of overall business performance.

 Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes multiple filters that allow users to analyze the data dynamically:

Brand – Analyze sales for different mobile brands.
Payment Method – Compare transactions based on payment methods.
Day Name – Analyze sales performance by day.
Mobile Model – Focus on specific mobile models.
Month Selection – View performance for individual months from January to December.

These filters make the dashboard interactive and allow users to drill down into specific segments.

 Geographic Analysis

The map visualization displays the geographical distribution of mobile sales across different locations.

It helps identify:

Locations generating higher sales
Regional sales distribution
Areas with stronger customer demand
 Total Units Sold by Month and Day

The line chart shows daily unit sales across the selected month.

It helps identify:

Daily sales trends
High-performing days
Low-performing days
Changes in demand throughout the month
📱 Total Sales by Mobile Model

The bar chart compares sales generated by different mobile models, such as:

Vivo S1
Vivo V20
Vivo E7

This visualization makes it easier to compare model-wise sales performance.

💳 Transactions by Payment Method

The pie/donut chart breaks down transactions according to payment methods, including:

Debit Card
Cash
UPI
Other payment methods

This provides an overview of customer payment preferences.

 Customer Rating Analysis

The customer rating visual displays the distribution of customer ratings and the number of customers associated with each rating.

It helps understand:

Customer satisfaction levels
Most common ratings
Distribution of customer feedback
Total Sales by Day

The sales trend chart shows how total sales change from day to day.

This can help identify:

Sales peaks
Low-sales periods
Daily fluctuations
Potential patterns in customer purchasing behavior
 Units Sold by Day

The final chart tracks the number of mobile units sold each day, helping compare daily product demand and identify changes in sales volume.
